# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in multilogin-labs a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Standards

**Encouraged:** respectful technical debate, reproducible evidence, clear disclosures on affiliate or sponsored content.

**Not allowed:** harassment, hate speech, doxxing, or encouragement of illegal activity.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **admin@multilogin-labs.github.io**. Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict access for violations.
